work

Systems in production

Each project started as a manual job someone did every day. Here is what it was and what replaced it.

1

Missed-call booking

n8n · Twilio · WhatsApp Cloud API · Google Calendar · Sheets

Before: a service business lost jobs every time the phone rang during a job and nobody picked up.

After: a missed call sends an instant SMS, continues the conversation on WhatsApp and books a slot straight into the calendar, with every lead logged to a sheet.

2

Product RAG assistant

n8n · Supabase · vector search · LLM

Before: customers asked the same product questions all day and staff looked up answers by hand.

After: one workflow keeps the product catalogue indexed, and an AI agent answers questions from it with the right models, prices and specs.

3

Attendance automation

n8n · Google Sheets

Before: attendance was collected and checked manually, and reports were late.

After: attendance is captured, validated and reported automatically for the client's team.

4

AI lead qualification

n8n · Grok · X

Before: sales time was spent reading every inbound message to find real buyers.

After: incoming leads are scored by AI and only qualified ones reach the team.

5

Important email backup

n8n · Gmail · Google Drive

Before: critical emails from key senders lived only in one inbox.

After: every email from an important sender is saved to Drive as a PDF automatically.

6

Daily news digest

n8n · RSS · Grok · Sheets · Pushover

Before: an hour a day scanning news sites for anything relevant.

After: news is collected, scored for relevance by AI and delivered as a short daily digest.
